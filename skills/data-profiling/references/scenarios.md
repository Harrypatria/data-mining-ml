# Distribution and data-condition scenarios

Columns: how to detect it, impact on models and metrics, remedy, what to report. Metric effects name accuracy, ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, recall, precision and F1 where relevant. Every remedy that learns a statistic (quantiles, means, encodings, imputations) is fitted on training folds inside the pipeline.

| # | Scenario | Detect | Impact on models and metrics | Remedy | Report |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Heavy right skew, heavy tails | Skewness above 1, mean far above median, log-scale histogram | Linear models and distance methods dominated by the tail; t-tests unstable; MAE versus RMSE disagree. Classification metrics shift little, but recall in tail segments can collapse | log1p, Box-Cox (positive only), Yeo-Johnson, quantile transform; tree models; rank tests | Transform used, before and after skew, metric by tail segment |
| 2 | Outliers | IQR or robust z-score (median and MAD) above 3.5, domain bounds | Coefficients and means distorted; RMSE inflated; isolated false positives raise FP and cut precision | Decide error or signal per rule; fix errors at source; winsorize or RobustScaler; Huber or quantile loss | Rule, counts removed or capped, sensitivity of headline metric with and without |
| 3 | Multimodal or mixed populations | Several peaks, mixture BIC, segment-wise histograms | One global model averages segments; calibration differs by segment; global ROC-AUC hides poor slices | Segment first, add the segment as a feature, mixture models, separate models when sizes allow | Metrics per segment with positive counts |
| 4 | Zero-inflated and count data | Share of zeros above Poisson expectation, variance above mean | Gaussian regression predicts negative counts; RMSE dominated by zeros | Poisson or negative binomial GLM, hurdle or two-stage (classifier for zero, regressor for positive) | Zero share, dispersion, model choice reason |
| 5 | Bounded and proportion data | Values in [0, 1] or percentages, mass at bounds | Linear predictions leave the bounds; heteroscedastic residuals | Logit transform with a small offset, beta regression, fractional logit | Bound handling, offset value |
| 6 | Missingness: MCAR | Missingness unrelated to any observed column (Little's test, logistic model on the mask) | Listwise deletion only loses power | Simple imputation; deletion acceptable if small | Rate, test result |
| 7 | Missingness: MAR | Mask predicted by observed columns | Deletion biases estimates; naive imputation shrinks variance | Iterative or KNN imputation inside the pipeline; multiple imputation for inference | Mechanism evidence, imputer, effect on metrics |
| 8 | Missingness: MNAR | Missingness depends on the unobserved value (domain knowledge, mask strongly tied to target) | Any imputation biased; mask itself is predictive and can leak | Missing indicators (`add_indicator=True`), sensitivity analysis, collect the data | Assumption stated, indicator importance, leakage check |
| 9 | Multicollinearity | VIF above 5 to 10, condition number, high pairwise correlation | Coefficients unstable and sign-flipping; SHAP and permutation importance split credit; prediction quality often fine | Ridge or elastic net, drop or combine features, grouped importance | VIF table, grouped attribution caveat |
| 10 | High dimensionality, small n | p close to or above n | Overfitting; selection on full data inflates every metric; CV variance large | Regularization, stability selection, strict nested CV, simpler models | Nested CV score with spread, selection stability |
| 11 | Small samples | n under a few hundred, or under 30 per group | Wide intervals; asymptotic tests invalid; single splits meaningless | Exact tests, bootstrap, Bayesian intervals, repeated CV, simple models | Intervals on every number, sample size per estimate |
| 12 | Noisy or weak labels | Label audit disagreement, confident-learning flags, inter-rater kappa | Ceiling on all metrics; precision and recall both understated or overstated by systematic noise | Relabel a sample, confident learning, robust losses, soft labels | Audit size, estimated noise rate |
| 13 | High-cardinality categoricals | Unique values above 50 or rare levels | One-hot explodes; naive target encoding leaks and inflates CV scores | Out-of-fold target encoding (`TargetEncoder`), frequency encoding, hashing, embeddings, group rare levels | Cardinality, encoder, leakage check |
| 14 | Duplicates and near-duplicates across splits | `duplicated()`, fuzzy matching, same entity in both splits | Leakage by repetition: test metrics optimistic across the board | Deduplicate, group-aware splits | Duplicate counts before and after |
| 15 | Temporal drift: covariate shift | KS test or PSI between periods, adversarial validation AUC above 0.6 | Random CV optimistic; live accuracy and recall lower than reported | Forward-chaining validation, recent-window weighting, monitoring | PSI per feature, adversarial AUC |
| 16 | Prior (label) shift | Prevalence differs between periods | Precision and PR-AUC move with prevalence at fixed recall; thresholds stale | Re-estimate prevalence, adjust threshold, recalibrate | Prevalence by period |
| 17 | Concept drift | Relationship between features and target changes (rolling metric decay) | All metrics decay over time | Rolling re-evaluation, retraining trigger, time-weighted training | Metric by period, trigger rule |
| 18 | Non-stationarity and seasonality | ADF and KPSS tests, decomposition, autocorrelation | Random splits leak the future; seasonal naive often wins | Differencing, seasonal features, forward-chaining CV, seasonal baselines | Test results, seasonal baseline comparison |
| 19 | Heteroscedastic, non-Gaussian residuals | Residual versus fitted plot, Breusch-Pagan, Q-Q plot | Standard errors and p-values wrong; prediction intervals too narrow | Robust (HC3) standard errors, GLMs, quantile regression, transforms | Diagnostics, interval method |
| 20 | Simpson's paradox and confounding | Direction of effect flips after stratification | Aggregate associations mislead; explanations attribute effect to proxies | Stratified analysis, adjustment, causal diagram | Stratified and pooled estimates side by side |
| 21 | Selection and survivorship bias | Sample excludes churned, rejected or failed units; data only after an approval step | Metrics valid only for the selected population; recall on the excluded group unknown | Define the scoring population; reject inference; collect unbiased samples | Population definition, excluded share |
| 22 | Class imbalance | Prevalence below 20 percent | Accuracy and ROC-AUC optimistic, precision falls with prevalence, default threshold wrong | See `model-evaluation/references/imbalance-and-metrics.md` | Prevalence and positive count next to every metric |
